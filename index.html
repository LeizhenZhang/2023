About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette . My research focuses on software engineering, software security, trustworthy artificial intelligence, and the evaluation of large language models.

My current research includes vulnerability detection, reliable and trustworthy AI for software engineering, large language model reasoning and evaluation, and machine learning for software and security applications.

Prior to joining the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Old Dominion University . I received my bachelor's degree from China Agricultural University .
